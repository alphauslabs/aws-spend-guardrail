# AWS facts

What the guard relies on, as confirmed in a test organization. Each entry says how it was checked. Entries marked "to confirm" are still assumptions.

| Fact | Status | How it was checked |
|---|---|---|
| SCPs must be enabled on the organization root before any freeze policy can be attached. A new organization may have them off. | confirmed | The test organization's root had no policy types. `enable-policy-type` moved it from `PENDING_ENABLE` to `ENABLED` within seconds and attached `FullAWSAccess` to the accounts. |
| An SCP rejects a wildcard in the service part of an action, such as `*:Describe*` in `NotAction`. | confirmed | Creating freeze style B with `*:Describe*`, `*:Get*`, and the like failed: "The provided policy document does not meet the requirements of the specified policy type." Style B now lists exceptions per service. |
| An SCP accepts a wildcard after the service, such as `ec2:Describe*`, and accepts the per-service style B list (273 entries, 4,883 characters). | confirmed | The policy was created with `create-policy` in the test organization, then deleted. |
| A CloudFormation `Rules` assertion using `AWS::AccountId` refuses a stack created in the wrong account before any resource is created. Rules are not evaluated when a change set is created, only when the stack is. | confirmed | `create-stack` with `ExpectedPayerAccountId` set to another account failed with "Parameter validation failed: assertion error" and no resource events. CREATE change sets with the same value were created without error. |
| Without a CloudTrail trail, Organizations account events (such as `MoveAccount`) do not reach EventBridge, although CloudTrail's event history records them. | confirmed | In an organization with no trail, an account was moved into a temporary OU and back. Both `MoveAccount` calls appeared in CloudTrail event history; an EventBridge rule on `source: aws.organizations` in us-east-1 matched nothing in 15 minutes (no matched events, no invocations, no failed deliveries). |
| With a trail recording management events, which EventBridge events arrive for account creation, invitation, and moves. | to confirm | Needed before relying on `ReactToAccountEvents`. |
| SCP size limit 10,240 characters; at most 10 SCPs attached directly to one root, OU, or account. | confirmed | AWS Organizations quotas documentation, checked 2026-11-11 (the limits were 5,120 and 5 earlier). Style B is within the size limit as created above. |
| An attached SCP takes effect within seconds. | confirmed | A deny on Bedrock invoke calls attached to a member account refused calls 1.3 to 3.4 seconds after `AttachPolicy` returned, and calls succeeded again as fast after `DetachPolicy`, over four rounds in the test organization on 2026-11-11. |
| A Budgets action of type `APPLY_SCP_POLICY` can target a member account. | confirmed | An `AUTOMATIC` action on e2estandard03's budget, targeting account 891376926337 with the freeze policy, reached `EXECUTION_SUCCESS` at 02:47:14 and 08:57:05 on 2026-11-12, and CloudTrail shows `AttachPolicy` by AWS Budgets' action worker (`InsightsActionWorker...`) at 08:57:05. |
| A Budgets action of type `APPLY_SCP_POLICY` can target an OU. | confirmed | An `AUTOMATIC` action on a test budget, targeting an empty test OU, reached `EXECUTION_SUCCESS` at 16:44:15 on 2026-11-13; its notification reported "attach policy p-z2u80hge to target OU ou-d0pk-7h1k65su", Success, and the OU listed the policy. |
| An action created on a budget whose spend is already over the threshold fires once Budgets calculates the spend, with nothing else needed. | confirmed | Test budgets with a 0.01 USD limit on an account with 8.29 USD of spend, created with their actions at 12:56 on 2026-11-13, read 0 until Budgets calculated them; the actions fired at 16:42 to 16:44, about 3 hours 48 minutes later. |
| A `MANUAL` Budgets action stays pending without attaching, and `ExecuteBudgetAction` with `APPROVE_BUDGET_ACTION` attaches it. | confirmed | A manual action went to `PENDING` at 16:42:39 on 2026-11-13 and attached nothing; approving it at 19:11:02 reached `EXECUTION_SUCCESS` within a second, and the OU listed the policy. |
| `ExecuteBudgetAction` with `REVERSE_BUDGET_ACTION` detaches the policy. | confirmed | Reversing the executed test action at 19:09:53 on 2026-11-13 reached `REVERSE_SUCCESS` within a second, and the OU no longer listed the policy. |
| What happens to an executed action at the start of a new budget period. | to confirm | A monthly test budget's executed action (`facts-tmp-manual`, attached to the empty test OU since 2026-11-13) is kept to read after 2026-12-01. The guard doesn't depend on the answer: if AWS resets the action, which detaches the policy, a `stay_frozen` freeze is attached again (fix-period-reset-unfreeze), and `auto_lift` freezes are lifted by the guard anyway. |
| At most 10 actions per budget and 100 budget actions per account, and 20,000 budgets per management account. Only 2 budgets with actions per account are free. Since each enforcing scope has one budget with one action in the payer account, a payer org can have at most 100 enforcing scopes. | confirmed | AWS Cost Management quotas documentation ("Quotas and restrictions", Budgets), checked 2026-11-12. The guide doesn't list these as adjustable. |
| How often Budgets refreshes actual cost. | partly confirmed | In the test organization, a new budget showed 0 actual for about 27 hours while Cost Explorer showed 5.24 USD, 2026-11-10 to 11. After an update, the same budget's action fired again about 4 hours 22 minutes later (update 22:25, `EXECUTION_SUCCESS` 02:47) and about 3 hours 17 minutes later (update 05:40, `EXECUTION_SUCCESS` 08:57), 2026-11-11 to 12. So expect hours after an update, and up to about a day for a new budget. |
| Creating or updating a budget resets its calculated spend to zero until AWS recalculates, and the caller can't set it. Its action can't fire in that time. | confirmed | AWS's `UpdateBudget` reference: "When you modify a budget, the calculatedSpend drops to zero until Amazon Web Services has new usage data." In the test organization, raising a frozen budget's limit from 4 to 8 USD took its actual spend from 5.744 to 0 at once, 2026-11-11, and new budgets read 0 for hours. |
| Updating a budget whose SCP action has executed resets the action, and the reset detaches the policy within a second. Updating a budget whose action is in `STANDBY` changes nothing about the policy. | confirmed | In the test organization on 2026-11-12, an `UpdateBudget` at 05:40:15 (no `UpdateBudgetAction`) on a budget whose action executed at 02:47 put the action in `RESET_IN_PROGRESS` at 05:40:15.893, and CloudTrail shows `DetachPolicy` by AWS Budgets' action worker (`InsightsActionWorker...`) at 05:40:16. The same call at 22:25 the night before, with the action in `STANDBY`, caused no reset. |
| `ExecuteBudgetAction` can't apply an action in `STANDBY`; its execution types only approve a pending manual action, retry, reverse, or reset. So the guard attaches a freeze policy itself when it must freeze before Budgets has spend. | confirmed | AWS Budgets API reference, `ExecutionType`. |
| The text of a budget notification and a budget action notification on SNS. | confirmed | Captured through an SQS subscription on 2026-11-13. A budget alert is plain text, subject "AWS Budgets: <budget> has exceeded your alert threshold", with a `Budget Name: <budget>` line. An action notification is JSON, subject "AWS Budgets Action: An action has successfully executed for <budget>", with `budget.budgetName`, `action.actionId`, `overallStatus`, and `action.taskExecuted[].taskDescription`. The guard reads the budget name from either form. |
| Bedrock runtime calls (`InvokeModel`, `InvokeModelWithResponseStream`, `Converse`, `ConverseStream`) are CloudTrail management events, free and recorded by default. | confirmed | AWS Bedrock CloudTrail documentation, and calls from a member account seen in event history 3 to 4 minutes later, 2026-11-11. Bedrock Agents, knowledge bases, flows, and async invokes are data events. |
| Non-streaming Bedrock calls carry `inputTokens`, `outputTokens`, and `inferenceRegion` in `additionalEventData`; streaming calls carry token counts on a separate completion record (`eventType: AwsServiceEvent`). | confirmed | Recorded calls in the test organization, 2026-11-11. |
| A call made with a Bedrock API key has `additionalEventData.callWithBearerToken: true`, with the key's IAM user as the caller, also through an organization trail. | confirmed | A temporary API key in the test organization, 2026-11-11. |
| An organization trail's S3 files hold member calls 0.5 to 5 minutes after the call. Through the trail's CloudWatch Logs group, 2 to 2.5 minutes. | confirmed | Four samples each, temporary organization trails, 2026-11-11. |
| CloudTrail events reach only the EventBridge bus of the account where the call happened, even with an organization trail, and no bus without a trail. | confirmed | EventBridge rules in the management and a member account, 2026-11-11. |
| A trail can't be narrowed to Bedrock management events: `eventSource` and `eventName` selectors are refused for management events; only KMS and RDS Data API can be excluded. | confirmed | `put-event-selectors` on a test trail, 2026-11-11. |
| Bedrock token-per-minute quotas can't be lowered below AWS's default. | confirmed | `request-service-quota-increase` with a lower value was refused, 2026-11-11. |
| AgentCore runtime, code interpreter, and browser calls are not management events. Only AgentCore Gateway is documented as a data event (`AWS::BedrockAgentCore::Gateway`). | confirmed | A code interpreter session (start, invoke, stop) in the test organization did not appear in event history after 4 minutes, 2026-11-11; AgentCore CloudTrail documentation. |
| A trail that logs `AWS::Bedrock::Model` data events records each runtime call twice: as a management event and as a data event, with different event ids and the same request id, time, request parameters, and token counts. `InvokeModelWithBidirectionalStream` and `StartAsyncInvoke` are data events only, and `bedrock-mantle` inference is a data event only (`CreateInference`, resource type `AWS::BedrockMantle::Project`); none of the three carries token counts, and no completion record follows. | confirmed | A temporary organization trail logging Bedrock and mantle data events, calls from a member account, 2026-11-17. |
| Bedrock token prices differ by region. Against us-east-1, the median of a region's prices: us-west-2 1.00x, ca-central-1 1.00x, ap-south-1 1.18x, eu-central-1 1.20x, ap-northeast-1 1.20x, sa-east-1 1.20x (up to 1.70x), eu-west-2 1.55x. `bedrock-mantle` prices outside us-east-1: median 1.20x, up to 1.60x. | confirmed | Price List entries for 8 regions, 2026-11-17. |
| A cross-region inference call is billed at the price of the region it was made from; global profiles cost about 10% less than geographic ones. | confirmed | AWS Bedrock cross-region inference documentation, checked 2026-11-17. |
| The Price List has Bedrock entries for 36 regions (34 commercial), 23,035 entries in all. Its API throttles beyond two concurrent paged reads; two at a time reads them all in 27 seconds. | confirmed | Timed from the test organization's management account, 2026-11-17. |
| `ListFoundationModels` and the mantle `GET /v1/models` list AWS's catalog for a region, not what an account has enabled. In a region the account hasn't enabled, `ListFoundationModels` is refused (`UnrecognizedClientException`), except that me-central-1's endpoint never accepted a connection. The mantle endpoint answered in 14 of the 18 enabled regions; in the other 4 its host doesn't resolve. Listing mantle models needs `bedrock-mantle:ListModels`, on a mantle project. | confirmed | Test organization, 2026-11-17; Service Authorization Reference for `bedrock-mantle`. |
| `bedrock-mantle` publishes `Inferences`, `TotalInputTokens`, and `TotalOutputTokens` per account and per model (and per project) to CloudWatch (`AWS/BedrockMantle`), in one-minute periods. The values matched the API's reported usage exactly for Chat Completions (plain and streamed) and Responses calls, and a datapoint appeared 24 seconds after a call. Refused calls aren't counted. | confirmed | Calls from a member account, 2026-11-17; AWS Bedrock documentation, "Monitor bedrock-mantle inference using CloudWatch metrics". |
| The organization's management account can be a CloudWatch cross-account observability monitoring account. With a member account linked, one `GetMetricData` `SEARCH` over `{AWS/BedrockMantle,Model}` from it returns one series per account, model, and metric, labeled with `${PROP('AccountId')}` and `${PROP('Dim.Model')}`. A new link took about 9 minutes to take effect, and for about a minute a direct query was refused while the listing worked. Datapoints from before the link appeared once the model emitted a new one. | confirmed | A sink in the test organization's management account and a link from a member account, 2026-11-17. |
| A root-attached SCP limited to one account by `aws:PrincipalAccount` blocks, in that account only: agents, flows, and knowledge bases by their actions; `StartAsyncInvoke` as `bedrock:InvokeModel` on `arn:aws:bedrock:*:*:async-invoke/*`, while a plain `InvokeModel` on the same model works; and a bidirectional stream as `bedrock:InvokeModel` on the Nova Sonic model. | confirmed | A test SCP in the test organization, calls from two member accounts, 2026-11-18; Service Authorization Reference for `bedrock`. |
| The Price List names mantle usage types by mantle model id (`USE1-google.gemma-4-26b-a4b-mantle-input-tokens-standard`) and prices OpenAI's dated snapshots (`openai.gpt-5.4-2026-03-05`) only under the base model's name (`OpenAI GPT-5.4`). | confirmed | Price List entries, 2026-11-17. |
| Bedrock logging-configuration changes are management events named `PutModelInvocationLoggingConfiguration` and `DeleteModelInvocationLoggingConfiguration`, recorded also when refused. | confirmed | Event history in the test organization, 2026-11-11. |
| A Bedrock call can choose a service tier: `priority`, `default`, `flex`, or `reserved`. `InvokeModel` and `InvokeModelWithResponseStream` take it in the `X-Amzn-Bedrock-Service-Tier` header, `Converse` and `ConverseStream` in a `serviceTier` field, and the OpenAI-compatible API (`/openai/v1` on `bedrock-runtime` and `bedrock-mantle`) in `service_tier`. Ultrafast, offered for some OpenAI models, is requested only through the OpenAI-compatible API, as `service_tier: "ultrafast"`. A model refuses a tier it doesn't offer with `ValidationException`. | confirmed | boto3 1.44.10's service model and calls on `openai.gpt-oss-20b-1:0` and Nova Micro from the e2e payer account, 2026-11-24; the GPT-6 Astra model card for Ultrafast. An Ultrafast call couldn't be made: GPT-6 Astra isn't enabled for the test account. |
| CloudTrail records the tier a Bedrock call used. `Converse` and `ConverseStream` carry `requestParameters.serviceTier.type`; `InvokeModel`, `ChatCompletions`, and the `InvokeModelWithResponseStream` completion record carry `serviceTier` in `additionalEventData` (or in the completion's `additionalEntries`). A call without a tier carries none. The `ConverseStream` completion record, which has the token counts, carries no tier; its `parentRequestId` is the request id of the `ConverseStream` call record, which has it. | confirmed | Priority, Flex, and default calls through each API from the e2e payer account, 2026-11-24, read with `lookup-events`. How an Ultrafast call is recorded is not yet seen. |
| Calls to the OpenAI-compatible API on `bedrock-runtime` (`/openai/v1/chat/completions` and `/openai/v1/responses`) are CloudTrail management events named `ChatCompletions` and `Responses`, with `requestParameters.modelId`, and for `ChatCompletions` `inputTokens` and `outputTokens` in `additionalEventData`. | confirmed | Calls from the e2e payer account, 2026-11-24. A `Responses` call that succeeds wasn't seen, nor a streamed one. |
| A streamed `ChatCompletions` call on `bedrock-runtime` leaves no token counts in CloudTrail. Its call record has `stream: true` and the tier but no tokens, and its completion record is an `AwsServiceEvent` named `InvokeModelWithResponseStream` whose `additionalEntries` carry `stream` and the tier but no tokens, linked by `parentRequestId`. A non-streamed `ChatCompletions` call record has the tokens. | confirmed | A streamed Priority call to `openai.gpt-oss-20b-1:0` with `stream_options.include_usage` set, from the e2e payer account on 2026-11-24; the response's last chunk had the usage, and no other record had arrived 5 minutes later. |
| `bedrock:InvokeModel` governs the OpenAI-compatible API on `bedrock-runtime`: a deny on it refuses `/openai/v1/chat/completions` and `/openai/v1/responses`, so the AI cutoff covers them. | confirmed | A test role allowing `bedrock:*` with the AI cutoff's actions denied was refused both calls, "not authorized to perform: bedrock:InvokeModel ... with an explicit deny", in the e2e payer account on 2026-11-24. The role was deleted afterwards. |
| Bedrock runtime publishes `Invocations`, `InputTokenCount`, and `OutputTokenCount` to CloudWatch (`AWS/Bedrock`) per model, and for a call on a tier also per `ServiceTier` and `ResolvedServiceTier` (the tier asked for and the tier that served it). A call on the default tier adds to the per-model series only. The metrics count calls CloudTrail leaves without tokens: a streamed `ChatCompletions` call's 5 output tokens appeared at 14:46. | confirmed | Calls from the e2e payer account on 2026-11-24, read with `list-metrics` and `get-metric-statistics` at one-minute periods. |
| How an Ultrafast call is recorded in CloudTrail. Likely `additionalEventData.serviceTier: "ultrafast"`, as a Priority call through the OpenAI-compatible API was. | to confirm | No e2e account can call a model that offers Ultrafast: GPT-6 Astra refuses the management account and a member account ("not available for this account ... contact AWS Sales") even with its Marketplace agreement accepted, 2026-11-24. The guard's daily `ultrafast_unseen` check reports a wrong guess. |
| What a successful `Responses` call on `bedrock-runtime` records, and whether it carries token counts. | to confirm | Only refused `Responses` calls were seen: `openai.gpt-oss-20b-1:0` doesn't serve that API, and GPT-6 Astra is refused to the e2e accounts, 2026-11-24. The guard treats a successful one without token counts as unmeasured. |
| `bedrock-mantle`'s CloudWatch metrics carry no service tier. | partly confirmed | The metrics seen on 2026-11-17 had only account, model, and project dimensions (the entry above on mantle metrics). Not checked since tiers came out. |
